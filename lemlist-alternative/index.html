Questions

How does pricing compare with Lemlist?

Asendify Outbound is priced in GBP: Base £29/month + 45% service fee (£42.05 total), and Pro £114.55/month all-in for 100,000 emails. Lemlist's Email plan is $69/month for 50,000 emails, and its Multichannel plan is $109 per user per month (2026 list prices).

Does Asendify Outbound do LinkedIn outreach like Lemlist?

No. Asendify Outbound focuses on cold email: unlimited inboxes, warm-up, sequences and an AI reply agent. If LinkedIn steps are essential, Lemlist's Multichannel plan covers that.

Can I import my Lemlist leads?

Yes. Export your leads as CSV from Lemlist and upload them to a campaign. Custom fields carry over as merge tags.
