Questions

Is Asendify Outbound cheaper than Woodpecker?

For steady campaigns, usually yes. At Woodpecker's listed $7 per 100 contacted prospects, 2,000 prospects a month is about $140 before discounts. Asendify Outbound's Base plan covers 2,000 contacts and 6,000 emails for £29/month + 45% service fee (£42.05 total).

Is warm-up included?

Yes. Warm-up runs on every inbox you connect, at no extra cost. Woodpecker includes 4 warm-ups and charges $5/month for each additional account.
