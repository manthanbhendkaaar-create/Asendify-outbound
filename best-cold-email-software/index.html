How to choose

  • Volume: each inbox should send about 20-50 cold emails a day, so sending 1,000 a day needs 20-50 inboxes. Pick a tool with unlimited inboxes and a flat price.
  • Replies: the money is made after someone replies. A tool that answers interested leads within minutes and shares your booking link turns more replies into calls.
  • Lead data: if you need a contact database, Instantly, Lemlist and Saleshandy bundle one. If you already have lists, you don't need to pay for it.
  • Pricing model: per-user pricing gets expensive as your team grows. Flat pricing doesn't.
