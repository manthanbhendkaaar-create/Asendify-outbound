Questions

Which inboxes can I connect?

Gmail and Google Workspace (with an app password), Zoho, and any provider that supports IMAP and SMTP over SSL (port 465). Outlook and Office 365 inboxes can't send from the platform yet.

How does the AI reply agent work?

When a lead replies, the agent reads it, sorts it and, if they're interested or asked a question, answers using only the offer details you give it and shares your booking link. Anything else is saved as a draft for you.

Is my inbox password safe?

App passwords are stored encrypted in a secure vault and are only used by the sending engine. You can remove an inbox at any time.

What is the 45% service fee?

Every Asendify plan and package carries a 45% service fee on top of the listed price. It is always shown before you pay: Base is £29 + £13.05 = £42.05/month, Pro £79 + £35.55 = £114.55/month, Scale £179 + £80.55 = £259.55/month.

What happens after the free trial?

The plan you picked starts automatically on day 15 and is charged through PayPal. Cancel any time before then in Settings and you pay nothing.
