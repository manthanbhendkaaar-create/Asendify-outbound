Every feature, by name

Everything Asendify Outbound does today, in plain words.

  • Unlimited email accounts: connect as many sending inboxes as you like, on every plan
  • Gmail and Google Workspace: connect with a 16-character app password
  • Zoho Mail: connect with an app-specific password, including zoho.eu and zoho.in accounts
  • Any IMAP/SMTP inbox: any provider with SSL (SMTP port 465, IMAP port 993)
  • Test button: check an inbox's connection from the Email accounts page
  • Daily sending limit per inbox: set from 1 to 200 emails a day for each inbox
  • Inbox rotation: sends are spread across your inboxes with safe daily limits and natural gaps
  • Warm-up network: your inboxes email each other and the network, switched on or off per inbox
  • Spam rescue: warm-up emails that land in spam are rescued automatically
  • Campaigns: create, start and pause campaigns, each with its own leads, sequence and settings
  • Multi-step sequences: add follow-ups and choose how many days after the previous email each one goes out
  • Same-thread follow-ups: leave a follow-up subject empty and it is sent as a reply in the same thread
  • A/B/C variants: up to 3 versions of each email in a sequence
  • Merge fields: {{first_name}}, {{company}}, {{website}} or any column from your CSV
  • Fallback values: {{first_name|there}} fills in a default when a field is empty
  • Spintax: {Hi|Hey|Hello} picks one option at random for each email
  • Sender name and signature: set per inbox; the signature is optional
  • Choose the sending inboxes: pick which email accounts send each campaign
  • Sending schedule: choose the days, the start and end time and the time zone for each campaign
  • Campaign daily cap: a maximum number of new emails per day for the whole campaign
  • Stop on reply: the sequence stops for a lead as soon as they reply
  • CSV lead upload: upload a CSV with an email column; first name, company, website and other columns become merge fields
  • Duplicate skipping: an email address already in the campaign is not added twice
  • Email checks: check your list before sending: valid, risky and invalid addresses are counted
  • Bounce and unsubscribe handling: bounces and unsubscribes are handled automatically to protect your domain
  • AI reply agent: switch it on per campaign to answer interested leads and questions
  • Offer facts: the AI may use only the facts you write about your offer: service, price, proof and who it's for
  • Booking link: the AI shares your booking link with interested leads
  • Reply sorting: every reply is labelled: interested, question, not interested, unsubscribe, out of office or wrong person
  • AI drafts: unclear replies are saved as a draft for you to edit before sending
  • One inbox for every reply: replies from all your email accounts in one place, with warm-up emails hidden
  • Reply from the inbox: answer any reply yourself without leaving the app
  • Campaign stats: leads, emails sent, replies, interested leads and bounces for each campaign
  • Encrypted app passwords: stored in a secure vault and used only by the sending engine
  • 14-day free trial: nothing is charged until day 15
  • Flat monthly plans: priced per workspace, not per user; cancel any time and your plan stays on until the end of the month you paid for

Questions

Which inboxes can I connect?

Gmail and Google Workspace (with an app password), Zoho, and any provider that supports IMAP and SMTP over SSL (port 465). Outlook and Office 365 inboxes can't send from the platform yet.

How does the AI reply agent work?

When a lead replies, the agent reads it, sorts it and, if they're interested or asked a question, answers using only the offer details you give it and shares your booking link. Anything else is saved as a draft for you.

Is my inbox password safe?

App passwords are stored encrypted in a secure vault and are only used by the sending engine. You can remove an inbox at any time.

What is the 45% service fee?

Every Asendify plan and package carries a 45% service fee on top of the listed price. It is always shown before you pay: Base is £29 + £13.05 = £42.05/month, Pro £79 + £35.55 = £114.55/month, Scale £179 + £80.55 = £259.55/month.

What happens after the free trial?

The plan you picked starts automatically on day 15 and is charged through PayPal. Cancel any time before then in Settings and you pay nothing.

How do I personalise each email?

Use {{first_name}}, {{company}}, {{website}} or any column from your CSV, with a fallback like {{first_name|there}} for empty fields. Spintax such as {Hi|Hey|Hello} picks one option at random for each email.

Can follow-ups go in the same thread?

Yes. Add follow-ups to the sequence and choose how many days after the previous email each one is sent. Leave a follow-up subject empty and it goes out as a reply in the same thread. The sequence stops for a lead as soon as they reply.

Does it come with a lead database?

No. You upload your own lists as a CSV. If you want us to build the list, the done-for-you package includes 1,000 leads.

Can I check my list before sending?

Yes. Check emails counts valid, risky and invalid addresses before you send, an address already in the campaign is not added twice, and bounces and unsubscribes are handled automatically.

Is the price per user?

No. Each plan is a flat monthly price for your workspace, not per user, and every plan includes unlimited inboxes, warm-up and the AI reply agent.

Does it connect to my CRM?

Not yet. Native CRM integrations, Zapier and webhooks, a public API, LinkedIn steps and separate client logins are not available yet.

Is there a free tool to plan my sending?

Yes. The free Cold Email Volume Calculator at outbound.asendify.co/cold-email-volume-calculator/ helps you plan your sending volume before you start.
