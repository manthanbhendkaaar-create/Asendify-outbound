Questions

Why does the number of inboxes matter?

A healthy cold email inbox sends about 20-50 emails a day. To send thousands a day you spread volume across many inboxes. Asendify Outbound allows unlimited inboxes on every plan; Mailshake allows 2 email addresses per user on its $49 plan.

How does pricing compare with Mailshake?

Asendify Outbound is £29/month + 45% service fee (£42.05 total) for the whole workspace with unlimited inboxes. Mailshake's Email Outreach plan is $49 per user per month with 2 sending addresses each (2026 list price), so it adds up as your team or volume grows.

Can I move my Mailshake leads?

Yes. Export your leads as CSV and upload them to a campaign.
