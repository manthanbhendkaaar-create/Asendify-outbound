An AI agent that turns cold email replies into booked calls

Most cold email tools stop at the reply. Asendify Outbound's AI reply agent picks up from there: it reads every reply, answers the interested ones in minutes, and sends your booking link, day or night.

Start free trialSee pricing

How it works

  1. Every reply is read and sorted: interested, question, not interested, unsubscribe, out of office or wrong person.
  2. Interested leads and questions get an answer in minutes, written only from the offer facts you give it, with your booking link.
  3. Unsubscribes are honoured and out-of-office replies are paused and retried later.
  4. Anything unclear becomes a draft in your shared inbox for you to send with one click.

Why speed matters

A lead who replies "sounds interesting" is warmest in the first hour. If the answer comes the next morning, many have moved on. The agent answers while you sleep, so more replies become calls.

It won't make things up

The agent only uses the facts you write about your offer. It never invents prices, results or clients, and when it isn't sure, it leaves a draft for you instead of sending.
