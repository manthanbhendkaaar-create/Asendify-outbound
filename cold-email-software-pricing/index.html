The costs on top of the plan

  • Sending inboxes: you pay your email provider (Google Workspace, Zoho or similar) per inbox, usually a few dollars a month each.
  • Domains: send from separate domains, never your main one. A domain usually costs around $10-15 a year.
  • Warm-up: some tools charge per warmed inbox (Woodpecker: 4 included, then $5/month each). Asendify Outbound includes warm-up on every inbox.
  • AI replies: some tools charge credits per AI reply or sell AI as a separate plan. Asendify Outbound includes its AI reply agent.
  • Seats: per-user pricing multiplies with your team. Flat pricing doesn't.

Example budget: 10,000 emails a month

10,000 emails over about 22 sending days is roughly 450 emails a day. At about 30 emails per inbox per day, that's around 15 inboxes on 5 domains. What hidden costs should I expect?

Sending inboxes from your email provider, sending domains, extra warm-up fees on some tools, AI reply credits, lead data credits and per-seat pricing.

How many inboxes do I need?

Plan on about 30 cold emails per inbox per day. 10,000 emails a month is roughly 15 inboxes; 100,000 a month is roughly 150.
