How many inboxes do I need for cold email?

Divide your monthly emails by about 660 (30 emails a day × 22 sending days) to get inboxes, then by 3 to get domains. 10,000 emails a month needs about 16 inboxes on 6 domains and about 3,334 new leads (1 email + 2 follow-ups each).

How the numbers are worked out

ResultFormulaExample (10,000 a month)
Emails per inbox per monthEmails per inbox per day × sending days30 × 22 = 660
InboxesMonthly emails ÷ emails per inbox per month, rounded up10,000 ÷ 660 = 15.2 → 16
DomainsInboxes ÷ inboxes per domain, rounded up16 ÷ 3 = 5.3 → 6
New leads per monthMonthly emails ÷ emails per lead, rounded up10,000 ÷ 3 = 3,334
Emails per sending dayMonthly emails ÷ sending days10,000 ÷ 22 ≈ 455

Rounding up matters: 15.2 inboxes means 16 real inboxes, or some of them go over your daily limit. If you want headroom for bounces or a paused inbox, add one or two spare inboxes.

Questions

How many cold emails can one inbox send per day?

A common safe pace is about 30 cold emails per inbox per day once the inbox is warmed up, which is the default in this calculator. Sending far more from one inbox raises the risk of landing in spam, so teams add more inboxes instead of pushing one harder.

How many inboxes do I need to send 10,000 cold emails a month?

About 16. At 30 emails a day over 22 sending days, one inbox sends 660 emails a month, and 10,000 ÷ 660 = 15.2, rounded up to 16 inboxes. At 3 inboxes per domain that is 6 sending domains.

Why use several inboxes per domain instead of one domain?

Spreading sending across a few inboxes per domain, and a few domains, keeps each one at a modest volume and limits the damage if one domain's reputation drops. Many cold emailers use separate sending domains so their main company domain is protected.

How many leads do I need per month?

Divide your monthly emails by the number of emails each lead receives. With a first email and 2 follow-ups (3 emails per lead), 10,000 emails a month needs about 3,334 new leads a month.

Can I send from a brand-new inbox straight away?

It is safer not to. New inboxes and domains are usually warmed up for a few weeks first, starting with small daily volumes, before they send cold email at full pace. Build that time into your plan.

Does this calculator guarantee my emails reach the inbox?

No. It does the arithmetic for volume, inboxes, domains and leads. Inbox placement also depends on authentication (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), list quality, bounce rate, copy and how recipients respond.
