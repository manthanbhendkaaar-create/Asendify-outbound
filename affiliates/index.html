Questions

How much do affiliates earn?

30% of every monthly payment from each customer you refer, for as long as they stay subscribed. A Pro customer pays £114.55 a month, so you earn £34.37 every month they stay.

When and how do I get paid?

Once a month via PayPal. Each commission is held for 30 days after the customer's payment, then paid in the next monthly payout.

60 days. If someone clicks your link and starts a free trial within 60 days, they are your referral, and you earn on every payment they make after the trial.
