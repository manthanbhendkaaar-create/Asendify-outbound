Questions

How does pricing compare with Reply.io?

Asendify Outbound is £29/month + 45% service fee (£42.05 total), flat for the whole workspace. Reply.io's Email Volume plan starts at $59 per user per month and its AI SDR plans start at $800/month (2026 list prices).

Does Asendify Outbound have an AI SDR?

It has an AI reply agent: it reads every reply, sorts it, answers interested leads and questions using only your offer facts, and shares your booking link. It doesn't write prospect research or run LinkedIn steps.

Can I import from Reply.io?

Yes. Export your contacts as CSV and upload them to a campaign.
